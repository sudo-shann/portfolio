Spec-first REST APIs (OpenAPI 3.0.3 on Flask and Connexion) that sit between monitoring and paging. Teams schedule and approve maintenance exceptions, incoming alerts are matched against the active ones, and matching xMatters events are annotated and terminated. An LLM layer classifies alerts and infers application context, and anything unmatched stays active and is returned as escalated.
